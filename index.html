Our hiring process

Three weeks, four fixed checkpoints

DAY 0 Calibration. Ninety minutes with the hiring manager. We leave with a scorecard, not a job description.
DAY 5 Market map. Every viable company and candidate cohort, sized and ranked, with real Bay Area compensation attached.
DAY 12 Shortlist. Five to seven candidates, each with a written assessment against the scorecard.
DAY 21 Close. We run the offer conversation, the counter offer risk and the notice period, then stay through onboarding.
